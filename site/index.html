Frequently asked

Is my ad account data safe?

TovaAds only accesses accounts you explicitly authorize via official platform OAuth. Tokens are encrypted at rest with minimal permissions, and all data belongs to you — export or disconnect anytime.

Which platforms are supported?

Facebook (Marketing API) and TikTok (Business API), with more planned. Both platforms share the same interface, rules engine and reporting.

Can the automation pause my ads by mistake?

Every rule is explicit — you define thresholds, actions and learning-period protection. The engine verifies each pause against the platform and alerts you via Telegram or in-app notifications.

Do landing pages have to use your domain?

No. Landing pages publish to your own domains — TovaAds never appears in your campaign URLs.
